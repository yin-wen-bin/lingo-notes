## Fonts

The nine bundled font families are DM Sans, Zen Kaku Gothic New, Noto Sans SC, Lora, LXGW WenKai Screen, Klee One, Literata, Noto Serif SC and Noto Serif JP. Their individual SIL Open Font License texts, copyright notices and provenance are preserved in `shared/typography/licenses/`, `shared/typography/README.md` and `shared/typography/manifest.json`. These files are also included in builds. Font files are not relicensed under this project's MIT license.
